# AstroGraph R22 — CURRENT Research Backlog

Document status: `CURRENT_R22_RESEARCH_BACKLOG`.

- acquisition of genuinely new OPS-SAT-like and ESA-like holdout material whose source bytes were not observed during R20/R21;
- external provenance demonstrating physical/statistical independence, not merely content/group identity novelty;
- a governed raw-to-5-minute ESA preprocessor that can be independently re-executed from new raw mission archives instead of accepting an upstream preprocessing receipt;
- independent provenance for the ESA upstream preprocessing implementation/runtime; R22 binds the consumed array bytes but deliberately does not prove raw-to-preprocessed equivalence;
- DXC remediation execution: R21 preregistered two DXC development candidates but did not select one, so DXC is not admitted to R22 replication;
- independent replication of any OPS/ESA development improvement before promotion or release-oriented interpretation;
- HIL, target-compute, operational-shadow and flight qualification;
- real 38×8 A0–A7 confirmatory LLM evidence and any Probe formal-EvidenceFact promotion.

Seen R20/R21 evaluation material remains read-only historical evidence and must not be reopened for development selection.
